# Business Insights – Calculated Fields

## Calculated Fields Created

### 1. Profit Margin

**Formula**

Profit ÷ Sales

**Purpose**

Profit Margin measures the percentage of sales retained as profit after accounting for costs. It helps leadership evaluate overall business profitability and compare financial performance across regions, categories, and customer segments.

---

### 2. Cost

**Formula**

Sales − Profit

**Purpose**

The Cost field estimates the cost associated with generating sales revenue. It provides insight into operational expenses and supports profitability analysis.

---

### 3. Average Order Value

**Formula**

Sales ÷ Number of Orders

**Purpose**

Average Order Value (AOV) measures the average revenue generated per customer order. This metric helps evaluate customer purchasing behavior and identifies opportunities to increase basket size through cross-selling and upselling.

---

### 4. Return Rate

**Formula**

Returned Orders ÷ Total Orders

**Purpose**

Return Rate measures the proportion of customer orders that were returned. A high return rate may indicate product quality issues, shipping problems, or customer dissatisfaction and should be monitored as a business risk.

---

### 5. Shipping Delay Bucket

**Logic**

Orders are categorized into:

* Fast (0–2 Days)
* Standard (3–5 Days)
* Delayed (6+ Days)

**Purpose**

This calculated field groups deliveries based on shipping time, enabling leadership to evaluate shipping efficiency, identify operational delays, and improve customer service.

---

## Business Value

These calculated fields enhance the executive dashboard by providing meaningful business metrics beyond the original dataset. They enable leadership to monitor profitability, customer purchasing behavior, operational efficiency, shipping performance, and return patterns, supporting informed and data-driven decision-making.
